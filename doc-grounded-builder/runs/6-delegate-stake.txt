» How do I stake PROS with a validator as a delegator to earn rewards?

1. PLAN (Docs MCP)
  The docs answer:
    **The available docs do not document a delegator staking flow**, so they don’t provide a valid delegation transaction or command to earn delegator rewards on Pacific Mainnet.
    
    The [Node Management](/node-and-validator-guide/node-management) gives the staking-contract address as `0x4100000000000000000000000000000000000000`. But its documented payable method is `registerValidator`—for validator registration, not delegator staking. It says delegator stake counts toward the validator’s 1,000,000 PROS activation threshold; it does not document how to submit that stake.
    
    The docs also don’t specify a delegator function or ABI, validator lookup/selection procedure, reward calculation or claiming process, or delegation withdrawal/unstaking rules. The [Validator Accountability](/node-and-validator-guide/validator-accountability) describes transaction-fee rewards for participating validators, not delegator rewards.
    
    You can verify your RPC is on Pacific Mainnet with this runnable command. **It only checks the chain; it does not stake:**
    
    ```sh
    cast chain-id --rpc-url https://rpc.pharos.xyz
    ```
    
    The expected chain ID is `1672`, per [Pacific Mainnet](/getting-started/network/pacific-mainnet). I can’t give you a safe, valid `cast send` or JSON-RPC staking request from the documented information without inventing the delegation method and calldata.
