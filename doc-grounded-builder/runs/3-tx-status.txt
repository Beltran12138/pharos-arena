    No contract address is needed. Query the receipt on the documented Pacific Mainnet RPC, then check its `status`.
    
    ```sh
    curl -sS https://rpc.pharos.xyz \
      -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
      --data '{"jsonrpc":"2.0","method":"eth_getTransactionReceipt","params":["0xa57f932d75c8fd7231e9da9c6d087dcd0a0c4eb6e6028500dba070965b8c9720"],"id":1}'
    ```

    * `"status":"0x1"` means the transaction succeeded.
    * `"status":"0x0"` means it failed.
    * `"result":null` means no receipt was returned. The docs don’t specify why or give a retry interval.
